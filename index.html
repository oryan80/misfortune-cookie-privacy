Privacy Policy

Misfortune Cookie
Effective date: October 13, 2026

Misfortune Cookie is a light-hearted app that serves a daily misfortune from Crumbs. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the app.

Information the app stores

The app does not ask you to create an account or enter your name, address, or other personal details. It stores app preferences needed to provide the daily fortune experience—such as the selected fortune and date—locally on your device using the device's app storage.

Advertising and third-party services

Misfortune Cookie uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google and its advertising technology may collect or receive information from the app and your device, such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and information used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. The exact information used can depend on your device, settings, region, and the advertising features enabled.

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob documentation.

How information is used

Information stored by the app is used to remember your daily fortune experience. Information handled by the advertising service may be used to provide and measure ads, maintain service security, prevent fraud, and support the advertising service, subject to Google's policies and your device or regional privacy settings.

Sharing

The app developer does not sell personal information collected directly through the app. Information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads as described above to provide advertising and related services.

Data retention and deletion

App preferences remain in the app's local storage until they are changed or removed. You can generally remove this local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and settings.

Children and families

Misfortune Cookie is designed to offer light-hearted entertainment. Because advertising services may handle device and usage information, parents and guardians should review the privacy information and controls provided by Google and their device platform. The app's advertising setup and store audience settings must be configured appropriately for the intended audience before release.

Security

The app uses local device storage for its saved fortune preferences. Google describes its own security practices in its privacy and service documentation. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You can manage available advertising and privacy controls through your device settings and, where available, Google's ad settings. Available options vary by device and region.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.
